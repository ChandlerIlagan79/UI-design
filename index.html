About KLD

Building the foundation for the Dasmarineños

Overview

Vision, mission, background, core values, institutional goals, and hymn

Vision ★

A transformative institution of higher learning that strategically contributes to the knowledge-based economy of the City of Dasmarinas and proactively responds to the needs of the community.

Mission ★

Committed to promoting quality and equity in higher education that upholds transformative leadership, community-benefited outputs, and research-oriented academic programs imbued with the onward-forward of the City of Dasmarinas

History

✦ Our background

A free-tertiary-education dream that began with a groundbreaking in 2018 and became a city college by ordinance in 2021.

It opened with a few programs, then grew to include nursing, engineering, life sciences, computer science, social work, and a midwifery degree. Graduates have earned strong results in licensure and certification exams.

Faculty are also active in research presentations, publications, and community linkages.

Core values

Knowledge

General goals

Feature highlight

Why Regals?

The college family takes its name from a large orange-and-black butterfly. It matches the city’s butterfly emblem and stands for transformation, self-trust, and coming back stronger.

Official logo

Each element carries a meaning tied to the college’s purpose.

  • Torch The vision to provide quality education to local youth.
  • Open book Sharing knowledge through transformative leadership.
  • Laurel wreath Success in developing well-rounded graduates.
  • Baybayin script Commitment to Filipino and local heritage.
  • Green and yellow Support for a green economy and sustainable living.
  • 2021 The year the college was established.
